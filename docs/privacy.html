Parent Privacy Notice

Version 3 · Effective October 18, 2026 · What changed

In short: only a parent or guardian can register a child. We keep the least information we need to run a free learning program, save your child's progress, and prepare certificates. Children never type personal information. We never sell information or use it for advertising. You can see your child's information in the Parent portal and delete it yourself at any time.

Who we are

Ewket Kids is a free learning program for children in preschool through Grade 6. It is run by Samra Ayele, a volunteer, in Columbus, Ohio. Samra is responsible for how information is handled here.

Using the lessons without an account

Anyone can use the lessons without signing up. Nicknames, animal icons, lesson scores, reading minutes, and the language choice are then stored only in that browser on that device. They are not sent to us. Anyone using the same browser can see them.

Who can register a child

Only a parent or legal guardian, age 18 or older, can create an account and register a child. Before registering, you confirm your email address, tell us you are the child's parent or guardian, and agree to this notice. Children do not create accounts.

We do not collect your child's email address, photo, voice, home address, school, phone number, location, or anything they type in their own words. We do not store answers to individual questions, only scores. There is no advertising, no chat, no analytics, and no tracking cookies.

Information from your child's device

When a parent signs in on the device a child uses, or links that device with a one-time code from the Parent portal, the scores listed above are sent with your child's student number. A linked device can only add scores for that one child. It cannot read anything back, see your account, or see other children. You can unlink a device at any time, and it stops saving immediately.

Who can see it

You can see only your own children, including the name you entered and their saved progress. The Ewket Kids organizer can look up one child's name at a time to prepare a certificate. That needs a password plus a phone code, and every lookup is recorded. The organizer's student list shows only student numbers, nicknames, and grades.

We never sell, rent, or share information for advertising. We share it with no one else unless the law requires it, or to protect a child's safety.

Certificates and public recognition

Certificates are given only to the child's family, printed or as a file. Your child's name is shown publicly, for example on a library honor display, only if you turn on "public recognition" on your parent page. It is off unless you turn it on, and you can change it at any time.

Your rights and choices

  • See it: the Parent portal shows your child's name, details, saved progress, quizzes, and linked devices.
  • Delete it: in the Parent portal, open your child's card and choose "Delete this child's information," or delete your whole account. You can also email us.
  • Correct it: email us from the address on your account to fix a name, grade, or age.
  • Stop new collection: unlink your child's devices and do not sign in on them. Your child can keep using every lesson.
  • Withdraw consent: delete your child's information or your account, or email us. We then stop using it.

We reply to emails within 30 days. Please do not email passwords or identity documents.

How we protect it

Only a parent with a confirmed email and an active sign-in can reach their own children's records; the database refuses everything else. The organizer's access needs a password and a phone code. Names never appear in web addresses, emails, or reports. Device keys and codes are stored only in scrambled form. We follow a written security program and review it at least once a year.

Changes to this notice

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will tell you in the Parent portal and by email, and ask you to agree again before using information in the new way.

What changed in version 3 (October 18, 2026): lesson progress and quiz results can now be saved to your account from your child's device; device linking is described; every outside service is listed; you can delete a child's information or your account yourself; and the time limits for each kind of information are listed.
