Parent Privacy Notice

Version 2 · Effective October 15, 2026

In short: only a parent or guardian can register a child. We keep the least information we need to run a free learning program and prepare certificates. Your child's name is private to you and the Ewket Kids organizer. We never sell it or use it for advertising, and you can delete it at any time.

Who we are

Ewket Kids is a free learning program for children in preschool through Grade 6, run by Samra Ayele in Columbus, Ohio. Questions: hello@ewketkids.org.

Who can register

Only a parent or legal guardian, age 18 or older, can create an account and register a child. Children do not create accounts and do not give us information themselves. Using the free lessons does not require an account.

What we collect when you register a child

InformationWhy we need it
Your email addressTo sign you in, confirm your account, and help you recover it
Your passwordTo protect your account. It is stored scrambled; we cannot see it.
Your child's full nameTo prepare certificates and honors, and to match the right record if you contact us
A nicknameShown on the student page instead of the real name
Grade and ageTo place your child at the right learning level
A student number (EW001 and so on)Assigned automatically so your child has a stable ID
The date you agreed to this notice, and your recognition choiceTo show when consent was given and what you allowed

We do not collect your child's email, photo, address, school, phone number, or location. Lesson progress stays in your own browser and is not sent to us.

Who can see it

You can see only your own children, including the name you entered. The Ewket Kids organizer can look up one child's name at a time to prepare a certificate. That requires a password plus a phone code, and every lookup is recorded. The organizer's student list shows only student numbers, nicknames, and grades. Our database provider, Supabase, stores the data on our behalf. We never sell, rent, or share your information for advertising.

Certificates and public recognition

Certificates are given only to the child's family, printed or as a file. Your child's name is shown publicly, for example on a library honor display, only if you choose "public recognition" on your parent page. That choice is optional, off unless you turn it on, and you can change it at any time. Registering a child does not by itself allow public display.

How long we keep it

We keep a child's record while they take part in the program. If you ask us to delete it, or if your account has no sign-in for 24 months, we remove the name, nickname, grade, and age. The student number is retired and never reused.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your child's information at any time by writing to hello@ewketkids.org from the email on your account. We reply within 30 days. You can withdraw consent the same way, and we will stop using the record. Please do not email passwords or identity documents.

Changes to this notice

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will tell you by email and ask you to agree again before using new information.
