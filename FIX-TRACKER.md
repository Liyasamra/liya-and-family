## Sharing reports

Create the link on the learning device, send it privately, and open it on the receiving phone. Nicknames, personal plans, and full history are excluded. Anyone with the link can read its encoded contents. Links are unsigned snapshots, cannot be revoked, and do not update. Full backups remain important until an optional parent service exists.

## Hosting decision still required

The GitHub connector in this session exposes source commits but no repository-visibility management operation. The account plan could not be confirmed through its available read interface. Do not switch visibility and assume Pages will keep serving: GitHub Free supports public Pages repositories; private-source Pages needs a qualifying plan. A private-source/public-built-output arrangement is another option, but must address the current repository's public history and preserve the chosen address. Making a repository private cannot recall copies already downloaded.
